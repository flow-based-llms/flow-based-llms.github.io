Floor Eijkelboom

PhD Candidate in Generative AI

I am a PhD candidate in generative AI at the University of Amsterdam, supervised by Jan-Willem van de Meent and Max Welling. My research centers on flow- and diffusion-based generative models. The main line of my work has been Variational Flow Matching — a simple, general, and efficient framework for learning flows over continuous, discrete, and geometric data. More recently I've turned to flow-based language models, which led to Categorical Flow Maps: VFM adapted for fast and controllable text generation. Alongside the PhD, I am an intern at CuspAI, working on foundation and generative models.
